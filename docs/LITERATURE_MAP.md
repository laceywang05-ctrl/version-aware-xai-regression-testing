# Literature Map

## XAI foundations

- Guidotti et al. (2018), *A Survey of Methods for Explaining Black Box Models*
- Barredo Arrieta et al. (2020), *Explainable Artificial Intelligence: Concepts, Taxonomies, Opportunities and Challenges toward Responsible AI*

## Explanation methods

- Ribeiro, Singh and Guestrin (2016), *Why Should I Trust You? Explaining the Predictions of Any Classifier* — LIME
- Lundberg and Lee (2017), *A Unified Approach to Interpreting Model Predictions* — SHAP
- Sundararajan, Taly and Yan (2017), *Axiomatic Attribution for Deep Networks* — Integrated Gradients
- Selvaraju et al. (2017), *Grad-CAM: Visual Explanations from Deep Networks via Gradient-Based Localization*

## Stability and critical evaluation

- Adebayo et al. (2018), *Sanity Checks for Saliency Maps*
- Alvarez-Melis and Jaakkola (2018), *Towards Robust Interpretability with Self-Explaining Neural Networks*
- Tomsett et al. (2020), *Sanity Checks for Saliency Metrics*
- Rudin (2019), *Stop Explaining Black Box Machine Learning Models for High Stakes Decisions and Use Interpretable Models Instead*

## Drift and ML testing

- Gama et al. (2014), *A Survey on Concept Drift Adaptation*
- Lu et al. (2019), *Learning under Concept Drift: A Review*
- Sculley et al. (2015), *Hidden Technical Debt in Machine Learning Systems*
- Breck et al. (2017), *The ML Test Score: A Rubric for ML Production Readiness and Technical Debt Reduction*
